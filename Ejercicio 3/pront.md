Design a web app for reading practice for people with dyslexia

Versions: two visual versions, one for children (ages 7-12) and one for adults.

Language: all interface text must be in Spanish.

General style

Minimalist, low distraction, strong visual hierarchy.
Soft cream or pastel backgrounds, never pure white.
Lexend typeface, large letters, generous letter and line spacing.
Clear icons accompanying every text label.
Large buttons with rounded corners.
Calm colors that convey trust and motivation.

Children’s version

Bright colors and illustrations.
A cute animal companion with a short, easy-to-remember name that guides the child and celebrates their achievements.

Adult version

Sober and clean design.
A more mature companion character with a supportive tone.

Screens

Welcome: choose between the children’s or adult version.
Initial test: a conversation with the companion, one question per screen, with a progress bar.
Adaptive reading: large text with an always-visible settings panel (font, size, spacing, background, reading ruler, voice button).
Exercises:
b/d letter discrimination.
Building syllables by dragging elements.
Multiple choice with audio.
Progress: progress bars and badges/medals, always with positive messages
